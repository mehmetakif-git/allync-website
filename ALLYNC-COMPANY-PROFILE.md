## 1.3 Executive Summary

ALLYNC is an **AI-first business ecosystem** that lets companies manage their customer communication, digital screens, and day-to-day operations from one connected platform — instead of stitching together a dozen disconnected tools. The ecosystem is built on three integrated platforms — **Allync Hub** (AI communication), **Allync Digital Signage** (smart screens), and **Allync+** (business management) — unified by a shared AI engine, one login, and one infrastructure, and extended through deep hardware, IoT, and enterprise integrations. ALLYNC is a **Meta-verified Tech Provider**, operates a **multi-tenant SaaS** model, and currently serves businesses across **Qatar and Türkiye**.

## 1.4 Company Overview

**ALLYNC is not just a company that develops a single piece of software — it builds an end-to-end AI Business Ecosystem for businesses.** Across integrated, AI-powered platforms, ALLYNC helps businesses manage their customer communication, their digital screens, and their entire day-to-day operations from one connected ecosystem.

The company operates on a **multi-tenant SaaS** model and serves businesses of every size — from small businesses to enterprises — across **Qatar and Türkiye**. **Allyncai** is the registered brand of ALLYNC. ALLYNC is a **Meta-verified Tech Provider**, building on the official WhatsApp Business Platform.

# Part 2 — The ALLYNC Ecosystem

## 2.1 What is ALLYNC?

ALLYNC is an end-to-end **AI Business Ecosystem**. Rather than selling isolated products, ALLYNC delivers integrated, AI-powered platforms that together run a business's communication, screens, and operations — from one login, one dashboard, one infrastructure.

## 2.2 Why ALLYNC Exists

Businesses don't need more software; they need software that works together. ALLYNC exists to replace fragmented, disconnected tools with **one unified intelligent ecosystem** where AI, people, devices, and operations work as one.

## 2.3 Problems We Solve

Modern businesses waste valuable time and resources managing **disconnected software** — separate systems for HR, CRM, Digital Signage, Customer Communication, Access Control, Employee Attendance, Bookings, Mobile Applications, and Analytics. Each requires **separate licensing, maintenance, training and support**.

ALLYNC eliminates this complexity: communication, workforce, operations, digital signage, IoT devices, and customer engagement are managed through **one intelligent ecosystem**.

## 2.4 One Platform Philosophy

> **One login. One dashboard. One infrastructure. One partner.**

Instead of purchasing, maintaining, and integrating multiple independent systems, businesses run everything through a single connected platform — where every solution works together by design.

## 2.5 AI-First Architecture

ALLYNC is **AI-native**, not AI-added. A shared AI engine, persistent memory, function calling, and AI agents run across the ecosystem — the AI learns the business, remembers context, makes decisions, and performs real actions. Intelligence is the foundation of the platform, not a feature on top of it.

# Part 3 — Allync Hub
### AI Communication Platform

## Overview

Allync Hub is a next-generation, **AI-native omnichannel communication platform** that lets businesses manage all of their digital customer communication from a single panel. It unifies channels under **one AI engine, one customer memory, and one set of workflows** — so no matter which channel a customer uses, the AI already knows the previous conversations and continues where it left off. Unlike traditional chatbots, it combines conversational AI with real-time business data, customer history, intelligent decision-making, and seamless API integrations. **Live on Web, Android (`com.allyncai.hub`), and iOS.**

## Omnichannel Communication

All channels share the same AI engine, the same customer memory, and the same workflows.

- **WhatsApp** — WhatsApp Business automation (Live). ALLYNC is a Meta-verified Tech Provider on the official WhatsApp Business Platform.
- **Instagram** — Instagram Messaging automation (Live).
- **Facebook Messenger** — Messenger automation (Live).
- **AI Voice** — Intelligent voice agents (in active development): operator-style phone calls that preserve the business's existing phone numbers.

## AI Memory

The AI learns the business's own knowledge — company information, products & services, prices, working hours, and company rules — and remembers **customer history** and **conversation history** across channels, building context from every new conversation for more accurate decisions.

## Customer Context

Every interaction is context-aware and personalized: the AI knows who the customer is, their history, and their intent, delivering a natural, business-specific experience.

## Workflow Automation

Appointment scheduling & calendar integration, order processing, lead qualification, broadcast campaigns (approved templates), and end-to-end business workflow automation.

## Function Calling

When action is needed, the AI **executes real operations** through Function Calling — not just replying, but doing (bookings, orders, lookups, updates) inside connected systems.

## Analytics

Real-time sentiment analysis and conversation analysis; live analytics across message volume, response time, sentiment, and resolution.

## Industries

Beauty & Wellness · Fitness & Gyms · Retail & Sales · Healthcare & Clinics · Hospitality · Professional Services · Customer Support Centers

## Enterprise Features

Multi-tenant SaaS architecture · team collaboration & conversation management · human handoff · 2FA · white-label & enterprise-ready infrastructure.

# Part 4 — Allync Digital Signage
### Smart Digital Signage Platform

## Overview

Allync Digital Signage manages **Android- and Windows-based screens** from a single cloud panel in real time. Its purpose is not just to publish content, but to **turn screens into smart devices**. Centralized single-panel management, one-click synchronized delivery to thousands of devices, real-time control, easy setup, and enterprise-grade scalability. Live on Google Play (`com.allyncai.signage`); works with the **Allync Dashboard** for centralized management.

## Device Management

- **Android** — Android TV, tablets, commercial display panels
- **Windows** — Windows-based signage devices
- Synchronized screen control, remote command sending, live device tracking, and device health telemetry (CPU, memory, network, proof-of-play, playback status)

## AI Generated Content

**Instant AI image/visual generation and publishing to screens within seconds** — AI-powered content generation and distribution.

## Playlists

Playlist management, central media management, multi-zone layouts (video, images, widgets), 12 built-in widgets, and a digital menu system (kiosk + auto-rotating TV mode).

## Music & Radio

Music & radio synchronization; server-synced background audio with per-track volume, crossfade, video ducking, and schedule timers.

## Synchronization

Real-time device synchronization over WebSocket; one-click synced content across thousands of screens; offline playback via local cache for uninterrupted operation.

## Sensors · Heatmaps · Touch Analytics

- Sensor integrations
- Heatmap analysis
- Viewer counting via **on-device face detection** (anonymous, no images stored, GDPR compliant)
- Touchscreen usage analytics

## NFC · IoT

NFC card read & write; integration with IoT devices as part of the connected ecosystem.

## Enterprise Deployment

DOOH ad system (programmatic injection, frequency scheduling, QR overlay tracking, CPV verified analytics), kiosk mode (PIN-protected admin), white-label branding, multi-language (EN/TR/AR), and scalable deployment across large device fleets.

# Part 5 — Allync+
### Intelligent Business Management Platform

## Overview

Allync+ is a **modular, cloud-based Business Management Platform** that lets small, medium, and large businesses manage all of their operations from a single platform. It brings a business's daily operations together under one panel.

Allync+ is **configured to each business**. On top of a shared core (HR, finance, CRM, sales, inventory, operations), **every industry receives its own tailored set of modules and features** — so a gym, a clinic, and a salon each get a platform shaped to how *they* work. The result is **ERP-level breadth — around 40 modules and a deep set of features per module** — while staying simple to use from one intelligent panel. Rather than a fixed product, Allync+ is a configurable operating system for the business.

## Human Resources

- **HR** — employee documents, certificate requests, employee self-service, performance & workforce management
- **Payroll** — salary and payroll processing
- **Attendance** — check-in / check-out, attendance tracking
- **Leave** — leave management
- **Shift Scheduling** — shift planning

## Business Operations

- **CRM** — customer relationship management
- **Sales** — sales management
- **Accounting** — accounting & invoicing
- **Inventory** — stock & inventory
- **Payments** — payments
- Calendar · Reports · Analytics · Role-Based Permissions · Notifications · Reminders

## Industry Modules

- **Beauty & Wellness** — Client Management · Appointment Scheduling · Treatments · Staff Scheduling · Sales · Inventory · Payments

_…and dozens more industry-specific modules._

## Customer Mobile Apps

# Part 7 — Security

Security and trust are among ALLYNC's strongest commitments — built into every layer of the ecosystem, not bolted on.

## Privacy

**Business data belongs to the business — not to us.** ALLYNC never sells, shares, or monetizes customer data. Your data remains your data. Privacy-by-Design architecture, secure data processing, and transparent data policies.

## GDPR · KVKK

GDPR Ready · KVKK Ready — designed with modern privacy regulations in mind.

## Data Ownership

Businesses remain in control of their information. Customers can **export their data, delete it instantly, request backups, manage retention policies, and control user permissions** — no unnecessary waiting periods, no hidden procedures, complete transparency.

## Regional Hosting

Businesses always know where their data is stored. ALLYNC supports **regional infrastructure** for data residency — e.g. Turkish customer data can be hosted in Türkiye; regional deployments are available on request (compliance + reduced latency).

## Authentication

- **MFA** — Multi-Factor Authentication
- **2FA** — Two-Factor Authentication
- **Face ID** — Face ID authentication
- **Fingerprint** — fingerprint authentication
- Email verification · secure API authentication · secure session management · device authorization · Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

## Encryption

End-to-End Encryption (where supported) and secure data processing across the ecosystem.

## Backups · Disaster Recovery

Automatic backups, disaster recovery planning, redundant infrastructure, high availability, continuous monitoring, and performance optimization for continuous business operations.

## Audit Logs

Audit logs and continuous security monitoring across the platform.
