Salon and spa management software lets you run your entire operation — from appointments to payments, from staff commissions to inventory — from one panel. Instead of calendar chaos, missed appointments and scattered client data, everything lives in one place.

In short

  • What it does: Unifies appointments, clients, staff, inventory and payments
  • Who it's for: Beauty salons, hair salons, spa & wellness, aesthetic clinics
  • Product: Allync+ Beauty & Wellness module (+ native customer and employee apps)
  • Highlights: Online booking, WhatsApp AI reminders, commission tracking

Core Features

Appointment Management

Online booking, calendar, double-booking prevention and automated reminders to cut no-shows.

Client Cards & History

Each client's service history, notes, preferences and package balance on one card.

Staff & Commissions

Staff scheduling, service-based commissions, performance and permissions.

Service & Treatment Catalog

Service list, duration, pricing and package definitions; campaigns and discounts.

Inventory & Payments

Product/stock tracking, sales, payments and invoicing; revenue reports.

WhatsApp AI Reminders

Automate appointment reminders, confirmations and rebooking with Allync Hub.

What to Look for in Salon Software

  • Online booking: Can clients book themselves?
  • Reminders: Automated WhatsApp/SMS reminders to cut no-shows?
  • Commissions: Are staff commissions calculated automatically?
  • Client history: Are service and preference histories kept?
  • One panel: Are booking, inventory and payments unified?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is salon management software?+
Software that runs appointments, client cards, staff commissions, service catalog, inventory and payments from one panel. The Allync+ Beauty & Wellness module delivers this with native apps.
Can clients book online?+
Yes. Clients book via a mobile app or link; double-booking is prevented and automated reminders are sent.
How do I reduce no-shows?+
With Allync Hub, automated WhatsApp appointment reminders, confirmations and rebooking significantly reduce no-shows.
Are staff commissions calculated automatically?+
Yes. Service-based commission rules are defined and calculated and reported automatically from sales/services.
Are inventory and payments in the same panel?+
Yes. Product/stock tracking, sales, payments and invoicing are unified with the booking system in one panel.
