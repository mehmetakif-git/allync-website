What is Gym Management Software?

Run memberships, class booking, staff, payments and member access (QR/NFC) from one panel — grow your fitness business with Allync+.

Home / Blog / Gym Management Software
Author: Allync Team Published: July 13, 2026 Read Time: 8 min

Gym management software lets a fitness business run all of its operations — memberships, classes/booking, staff, access control, payments and reporting — from one panel. Instead of paper member lists, separate payment tracking and manual door checks, everything lives in one place.

In short

  • What it does: Unifies memberships, classes, payments and access
  • Who it's for: Gyms, fitness studios, personal trainers
  • Product: Allync+ Fitness module (+ native customer and employee apps)
  • Highlights: QR/NFC access, automated payments, class booking

Core Features

Membership Management

Member records, package and subscription tracking, renewal reminders, freezes and cancellations.

Classes & Booking

Group classes, PT appointments, capacity management and automated reminders.

Access Control (QR/NFC)

Check-in/check-out with QR codes and NFC cards; integrates with Biostar door access systems.

Payments & Subscriptions

Automated billing, payment tracking, invoicing and revenue reports.

Staff & Trainers

Shifts, commissions, performance and role-based permissions.

Measurements, Programs & Analytics

Member measurements, workout programs, attendance and business analytics.

What to Look for in Gym Software

  • One panel: Are memberships, payments and access separate systems or unified?
  • Mobile apps: Booking/payments for members, access/shifts for staff?
  • Hardware integration: Turnstiles, Biostar doors, NFC cards supported?
  • Automated payments: Is subscription billing automatic?
  • Scalability: Can it grow from one location to a chain?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is gym management software?+
Software that runs a fitness business — memberships, classes/booking, staff, access, payments and reporting — from one panel. The Allync+ Fitness module delivers this with native customer and employee apps.
How do members check in?+
Via QR code or NFC card for check-in/check-out. It integrates with Biostar door access systems and turnstiles.
Can I collect payments automatically?+
Yes. Subscription and package billing can be automated; payment tracking, invoicing and revenue reports live in one panel.
Is there a mobile app?+
Yes. A Customer App where members book, manage membership and pay, and an Employee App for check-in/out, shifts and internal requests.
Does it support chains / multiple branches?+
Yes. Allync+ runs on a multi-tenant architecture; it scales from one location to a chain with role-based permissions.
