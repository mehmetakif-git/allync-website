What is Text-to-Video AI?

Text-to-Video AI is a system that uses artificial intelligence technology to automatically convert written text into video content. Allync's text-to-video solutions create professional videos with visuals, animations, voiceovers, and music from your script text.

Allync Text-to-Video Advantage

Allync's text-to-video AI system offers natural voiceover support in Turkish, English, and Arabic. It produces videos that match your brand's visual identity and style guide.

How Does Text-to-Video AI Work?

  1. Text Input: Enter your script or description text into the system
  2. AI Analysis: Allync AI analyzes the text and determines the scene structure
  3. Visual Generation: Appropriate visuals and animations are created for each scene
  4. Voiceover: Natural voice synthesis adds AI-based voiceover
  5. Music and Effects: Background music and sound effects are integrated
  6. Final Video: All elements are combined to prepare the professional video
