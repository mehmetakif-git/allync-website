Inegol/Bursa: Turkey's Emerging Software Hub

The Bursa software companies ecosystem has expanded visibly over the last five years. Bursa Technical University, Uludag University, and the TEKNOSAB technology zone produce a steady pipeline of skilled software engineers, while younger technology companies prefer to stay local rather than migrate to Istanbul.

Why Inegol?

The rise of Inegol software has concrete drivers:

  • Industrial density: Inegol is one of Turkey's largest furniture manufacturing centers and has strong automotive supplier, food, and metalworking industries — a natural digital transformation market for local software companies.
  • Talent base: Graduates from Bursa universities now have an option to stay in their region instead of migrating to Istanbul.
  • Cost advantage: Office rent, living costs, and operational expenses are significantly lower than Istanbul or Western Europe.
  • Communication speed: Bursa is under two hours from Istanbul, but in-person service for local customers is still easy.
  • KVKK compliance: A Turkey-based software company is naturally aligned with KVKK data-protection regulations.

The Size of the Bursa Software Sector

According to Bursa Chamber of Commerce data, Bursa is Turkey's fourth-largest economy. With a strong industrial base, technology spending grows at double-digit rates annually. Bursa software development companies are the most agile players in this market.
