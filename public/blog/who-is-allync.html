Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Who is Allync? +
Allync is an innovative AI technology company that develops WhatsApp AI assistants and custom SaaS solutions. Operating from Qatar and serving clients worldwide, Allync helps businesses automate customer communication and increase operational efficiency.
What does AllyncAI do? +
AllyncAI provides AI-powered WhatsApp assistants, custom SaaS solutions, business process automation, appointment scheduling systems, CRM integrations, and data analytics services. Our solutions serve industries including beauty salons, healthcare, legal services, restaurants, and retail.
How does Allync's WhatsApp AI work? +
Allync's WhatsApp AI assistant connects to your business WhatsApp number and handles customer messages automatically. It's trained on your specific business information, products, services, and policies. The AI can answer questions, book appointments, take orders, and escalate complex issues to human agents when needed.

Is Allync suitable for small businesses? +
Absolutely! Allync offers flexible packages designed for businesses of all sizes. Our Starter Package is specifically designed for small businesses, providing essential AI capabilities at an affordable price point. As your business grows, you can easily upgrade to more comprehensive packages.
How long does setup take? +
Basic setup can be completed in as little as 24-48 hours. This includes connecting your WhatsApp Business account, basic AI training, and initial configuration. More comprehensive implementations with custom integrations may take 1-2 weeks depending on complexity.
What languages does Allync support? +
Allync's AI assistants support multiple languages including English, Turkish, and Arabic. Additional languages can be configured based on your business needs. The AI can even handle multilingual conversations, switching between languages as needed.
