What is CRM Integration?

CRM (Customer Relationship Management) refers to customer relationship management. CRM integration is the process of connecting this system to work with other business tools and platforms.

With Allync's CRM integration solutions:

  • Customer information from WhatsApp AI is automatically saved to CRM
  • Sales processes are managed from a central system
  • Customer interaction history is collected in one place
  • Automatic reporting and analysis capabilities are provided

Allync CRM Integration Features

Allync's WhatsApp AI solution works fully integrated with your CRM system. Customer conversations, lead information, and sales opportunities are automatically transferred to CRM.
