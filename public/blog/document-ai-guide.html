What is Document AI?

Document AI is a technology that uses artificial intelligence and machine learning to automatically extract data, classify, and analyze documents. Going far beyond traditional OCR, it understands document context and produces structured data.

Example Scenario

Your accounting department processes 200 invoices daily. With Document AI, these invoices are automatically scanned, vendor name, invoice amount, VAT, date are extracted and transferred directly to your accounting software. A 4-hour job takes just 15 minutes.

How Document AI Works

  1. Document Upload: Documents in PDF, Word, Excel, image formats are uploaded
  2. Pre-processing: Image enhancement, skew correction, noise removal
  3. Text Recognition: All text content is extracted with advanced OCR
  4. Understanding: AI understands document type and structure
  5. Data Extraction: Relevant fields (date, amount, name, etc.) are automatically detected
  6. Validation: Extracted data is verified with cross-checks
  7. Integration: Data is transferred to ERP, CRM, or other systems

Benefits of Document AI
