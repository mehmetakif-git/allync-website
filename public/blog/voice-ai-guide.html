What is Voice AI?

Voice AI refers to systems that use artificial intelligence technologies to synthesize, recognize, and process human voice. Allync's voice AI solutions enrich customer experience by producing natural, human-like voices.

Voice Cloning Technology

Voice cloning is the process of learning and reproducing a specific person's voice by artificial intelligence. With Allync's voice cloning solutions, you can:

  • Digitize your brand representative's voice
  • Create announcements and messages with your CEO or founder's voice
  • Voice all your content with a consistent brand voice
  • Produce content with the same voice character in different languages

How Does Voice AI Work?

  1. Text Input: The text to be voiced is entered into the system
  2. Language Analysis: Allync AI analyzes the text to determine correct pronunciation and emphasis
  3. Voice Synthesis: Natural voice is produced with artificial neural networks
  4. Emotion Addition: Desired tone and emotion are reflected in the voice
  5. Optimization: Voice quality and clarity are optimized
  6. Delivery: Delivered as MP3, WAV, or real-time streaming
