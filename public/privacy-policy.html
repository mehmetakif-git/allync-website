5. AI-Powered Chatbot

Our WhatsApp assistant uses artificial intelligence to provide automated responses about Allync's services, pricing, and solutions.

5.1 How the AI Chatbot Works

  • Your messages are processed by our AI system to understand your intent and provide relevant answers
  • The AI is trained exclusively on Allync company information (services, pricing, features, industry solutions)
  • The AI does not have access to your personal accounts, financial data, or sensitive personal information
  • Complex inquiries are escalated to human representatives

5.2 AI Data Handling

  • Messages processed by the AI are used solely for generating responses and improving service quality
  • We do not use your conversations to train general-purpose AI models
  • AI-processed data is subject to the same security measures as all other personal data
  • You may request deletion of your conversation history at any time

8. Your Rights

Under GDPR and KVKK, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing: Request limitation of how we process your data
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object: Object to processing of your data for certain purposes
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw previously given consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@allyncai.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.
